## Demo workspace data

The FlowDesk intake, review, list and detail pages use session storage in your browser tab. Entered messages, drafts and changes stay in that session. Refreshing retains your work. Closing the tab normally ends the session.

## Input and sharing

Use redacted conversations. The demo processes messages in your browser without sending them to an AI provider. List filters appear in the URL. Clear filters before sharing a link if needed. Clear the site's browser storage to remove local changes.
