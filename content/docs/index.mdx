## Capture a real conversation

Open [Intake](/en/intake), then add the request context and raw team chat. Live input uses dark text; loaded example copy stays gray so it cannot be mistaken for your own data.

Paste timestamps, speakers, replies, and incomplete verbal agreements as they appear. Business line, message date, and special instructions stay under Additional settings until you need them.

> Keep the original order. Who asked first, who answered later, and who committed to follow up all affect the result.

## What to review first

Review keeps the full source on the left and the action draft on the right. Select a field to reveal the matching sentence without leaving the draft.

| Check                    | What to confirm                                             | FlowDesk behavior                               |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Owner                    | A clear commitment or assignment exists                     | Leaves the field empty when evidence is missing |
| Contact and participants | The person contacted may differ from the person responsible | Stores each role separately                     |
| Due date                 | The source gives a date or only a range                     | AI keeps the source phrase; rules normalize it  |
| Evidence                 | Every value links to a sentence                             | Opens the exact source fragment                 |
| Duplicates and conflicts | A similar action already exists                             | Explains the warning and asks you to decide     |

## Complex conversation handling

A meeting-planning thread can contain a proposed time, agenda deadline, material deadline, attendance reports, room capacity, and equipment checks. FlowDesk reads the thread in order: answered questions become decisions, unresolved questions remain open, and unfinished work becomes separate actions.

“I’ll check the display adapters” can create an owned action. “How many interface owners are coming?” remains an open question when the thread contains no answer.

## Track, close, and undo

Filter the [action list](/en/items) by business line, owner, date, or status. Open an action to inspect its source message, field evidence, and change history.

- Write: save a reviewed draft as an action.
- Merge: choose the target after confirming a duplicate.
- Close: require explicit completion or a manual confirmation.
- Undo: reverse the latest merge or closure from the detail page.

## Common questions

### Why is the owner missing?

The source may name only a contact or participant. Select an owner in review; FlowDesk does not substitute the speaker.

### Why is the date a range?

Phrases such as “this week” are not exact. FlowDesk shows the calculated range and asks you to confirm the final due date.

### Why did a complex thread produce no actions?

Keep speakers and message order in the pasted source. If the AI service is unavailable, FlowDesk shows local-rule results; retry later or complete the missing fields in review.

### Are drafts written immediately?

No. Every result enters review first and becomes an action only after confirmation.
