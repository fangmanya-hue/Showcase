title: FlowDesk guide
---

## Capture a conversation

Open [Intake](/en/intake), paste redacted messages and leave a blank line between actions. Choose the business line and message date, then generate drafts. The demo extracts supported fields in your browser.

## Review owners and dates

Open [Review](/en/review) and expand a field to see the highlighted sentence. Select an owner when shorthand has multiple matches. Confirm a due date when the source only describes a range.

## Track an action

Filter the [action list](/en/items) by business line, owner, date or status. Open an action to see its messages and change history. Confirm merges and closures explicitly and undo the last change from the target action.
